About CampusFind

A centralized, smart Lost & Found management ecosystem engineered specifically to solve campus loss recovery challenges.

The Core Problem

On university campuses, thousands of items (student ID cards, textbooks, keys, water bottles, and expensive electronics like laptops or earbuds) are misplaced each semester.

  • Fragmented Channels: Lost items are posted randomly on unorganized WhatsApp groups, unofficial Instagram pages, or physical notice boards where posts are quickly buried.
  • No Centralized Search: Students have no easy way to filter by location, category, or date to see if their item has already been turned in.
  • Fraudulent Claims & Privacy Risks: Anyone can claim an item falsely without proof, and finders hesitate to post their personal phone numbers publicly.

Our Approach

CampusFind provides an automated, privacy-first web application accessible across both desktop and mobile web browsers:

  • Intelligent Auto-Matching: A multi-criteria matching engine in Python evaluates new posts against open posts to alert owners automatically.
  • Cryptographic Verification: Finders configure a secret question. Claimants must answer correctly to unlock contact details, with a 3-attempt safety lock.
  • Privacy & Moderation: Contact details and verification answers are never exposed publicly, and administrative tools prevent misuse.

The Auto-Matching Algorithm (matching.py)

When any item is posted or updated, the system executes an automated compatibility calculation against all active posts of the opposite type. The score is calculated out of 100 points:

40 Pts
Category Match
Strict category equality
25 Pts
Location Match
Exact, substring, or token
15 Pts
Date Match
Within 3 days window
20 Pts
Keyword Overlap
5 pts per shared word
Match Threshold: Any pair scoring 60 points or higher is immediately registered as a verified match. The system records the match in the database and delivers instant in-app alerts to both the owner and finder.
